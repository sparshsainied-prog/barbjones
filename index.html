Fate of an Angel

Zaraquel was supposed to be the balance between bloodlines, the one thing that kept everything from breaking apart. Now she is gone, her light taken and sealed somewhere no one can reach, and whatever was holding the peace together is starting to give way. Marcus does not care what it costs anymore. He will go into hell itself if that is what it takes to bring her back.

In Hawai‘i, the mana is shifting, pulling something old to the surface that was never meant to return. As things begin to fracture, alliances stop meaning what they used to.

Power comes with a cost, loyalty is tested, and some choices cannot be taken back.

About Barb

Barb Jones is the award-winning author of The Blood Prophecy saga and the Heaven and Hell series. Her novels blend dark fantasy, supernatural horror and thriller elements, where angels, demons, ancient bloodlines and prophecy collide.

“I always keep to my roots when I write and I listen to the key people in my life: my family, friends, and fans.”
